#4471 closed on 5 October: the parser fix shipped and the booking-v3 form is retired. That engineering update does not mention the one-day moves, so their ticket stays open.

The problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. A tenant that writes the day first sees 7 April come out as 4 July. Reports come in by app, by email and through the booking-v2 form. Ticket mega-what-it-shows#1, fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. That fix closes this problem only.

hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. At tenants that write the day first, people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The result looks the same as parser, but it comes from the form, so the parser fix does not touch it. Ticket mega-what-it-shows#2, won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, and with it the hint.

One-day move: a booking lands one day off. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; one meeting is on Tuesday in Lisbon and on Monday in Toronto. The month and the day stay in place, so date order cannot explain it. It looks like a time zone problem. The table files these reports by tenant, under parser, and counts them there; the note under it names how many the desk read this way. Ticket mega-what-it-shows#3, still open: neither the parser fix nor the retired form explains a booking that moves by one day.

#4471 is closed, but the one-day moves are not fixed. Keep their ticket open until a fix ships.

How they ended up in one ticket

Decision 0001 merges reports on the subject line alone. “Booking on the wrong date” reads the same for every problem here, so nobody read the bodies. Intake’s tag does not split them either: it is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and the “tagged as” column shows how far that guess is from the count by tenant and channel.

How the table counts

  • One id, one report. The export comes in overlapping pages, so some reports appear twice.
  • A report’s cause comes from its tenant (locale and booking form) and its channel, as causes.ts decides it. The reporter’s browser does not count.
  • A report the desk read as a one-day move is counted where it is filed, and named in the note.
